# User Interview — Japanese YouTube → JLPT Flashcard Generator

- **Who:** Team 6 members (8 members)
- **When:** 2026/06/21
- **How:** Google meet meeting (~1 hour 30 mins)

## What they do today (without your project)

<!-- How do they handle this now? 2-3 lines. -->

Currently, they have to manually pause videos, look up unknown words one by one, write down vocabulary lists, and guess JLPT levels. The extraction process takes longer than actually watching the video.

## What they liked

- **JLPT Flashcard Leveling:** Loved how cards are automatically assigned difficulty levels for structured study.
- **Ease of Input:** Appreciated being able to just paste a YouTube link or raw text to generate an entire deck instantly.
- **Customization Capability:** Big fan that users can customize the generated flashcards themselves rather than being stuck with rigid defaults.
- **UI Design:** The visual styling and presentation of the flashcards were well-received.

## What confused them / what's missing

- **Input UX Clarity:** A bit confused about _where_ exactly an end-user is supposed to paste the YouTube link on the interface.

## What would make them actually use it

- Clearer primary input field (prominent search/link bar on the landing page) so users instantly know where to paste their YouTube URL or text.

## What I'll change (next steps)

- [ ] Redesign the main landing page hero section to make the YouTube link / text input box prominent and obvious.
- [ ] Add explicit placeholder text or micro-copy (e.g., _"Paste YouTube URL or raw text here..."_) to guide first-time users.
- [ ] Ensure the card customization tools (editing definitions, levels, and readings) are intuitive and easy to access post-generation.
